What is a Word Frequency Counter?

A word frequency counter calculates how many times each unique word appears in a text document. It calculates percentage ratios and keyword density for SEO content optimization, copywriting, and linguistics.

Why Ignore Stop Words?

Stop words are high-frequency grammatical connector words like the, is, in, at, that, and. Filtering stop words highlights the core subject keywords and topics in your content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is keyword density calculated?

Keyword density is calculated by dividing individual word occurrences by the total filtered word count.

Can I filter out short 1-letter or 2-letter words?

Yes. Adjust the "Min Word Length" field to filter out words shorter than your preferred character count.
