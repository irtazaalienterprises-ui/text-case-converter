How Reading Time is Calculated

Reading time is calculated by dividing total word count by reading speed in words per minute (WPM). The average adult reads non-fiction at approximately 200 to 250 WPM.

Average Reading Speeds Overview

- Slow Reader / Proofreader: 100 - 130 WPM
- Average Adult: 200 - 230 WPM
- Fast Adult Reader: 250 - 300 WPM
- Public Speaking / Presentations: 130 - 150 WPM

Frequently Asked Questions

What is WPM?

WPM stands for Words Per Minute. It is the standard unit used to measure reading and typing speed.

Why is speaking time different from reading time?

People naturally read silently much faster than they speak out loud. Speech requires pauses, inflection, and deliberate pronunciation (approx. 130 WPM).
