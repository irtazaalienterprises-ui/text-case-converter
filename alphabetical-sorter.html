What is an Alphabetical Order Sorter?

An alphabetical order sorter organizes text lists into alphabetical sequence (A-Z or Z-A). It is widely used by students, researchers, email marketers, and programmers to organize itemized lists, references, keywords, or directories.

Key Features

- A to Z & Z to A Sorting: Alphabetize text in standard or reverse alphabetical order.
- Remove Duplicates: Automatically filter out duplicate entries.
- Sort by Length: Arrange items from shortest to longest line length.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does case-insensitive sorting work?

Case-insensitive sorting treats capital and lowercase letters equally so "apple", "Apple", and "APPLE" are sorted together.

Can it remove duplicate items from my list?

Yes. Simply keep "Remove Duplicate Lines" checked to strip duplicate entries automatically.
