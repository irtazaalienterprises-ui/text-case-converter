Why Use an Online Word Counter?

An online word counter is an essential writing assistant for students, writers, SEO specialists, bloggers, and copywriters. It ensures your writing meets targeted length requirements for articles, blog posts, essays, and assignments.

Recommended Word Length Guidelines

- Blog Post (SEO): 1,500 to 2,500 words for comprehensive search rankings.
- College Essay / Paper: 500 to 2,000 words.
- Cover Letter: 250 to 400 words.
- Short Story: 1,000 to 7,500 words.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Word Counter calculate total words?

Words are detected by splitting text along whitespace characters while recognizing hyphenated terms and standard word boundaries.

What is unique word count?

Unique words measure the variety of vocabulary used in your text by ignoring duplicate occurrences of words.

Is my content stored or sent anywhere?

No. All calculations run strictly in your web browser for complete privacy.
