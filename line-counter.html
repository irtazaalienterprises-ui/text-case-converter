What is a Line Counter?

A line counter counts newline breaks (`\n`) in a block of text, code, or list. It distinguishes between total lines, filled lines, and blank lines to help developers, writers, and data analysts verify line counts.

Useful Features

- Total Lines: Counts every line separated by enter/return keys.
- Non-Empty Lines: Counts lines containing actual text characters.
- Line Numbering: Prepends numbers (1., 2., 3.) to each line automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are empty lines calculated?

Empty lines are lines that contain no text or only whitespace characters.

Can I add or remove line numbers?

Yes. Use the "Toggle Line Numbers" button to instantly number or un-number every line in your document.
