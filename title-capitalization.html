What is Title Capitalization?

Title capitalization standardizes article titles, blog headlines, book titles, and email subject lines according to editorial guidelines like AP Style, Chicago, APA, or MLA.

Title Capitalization Rules Summary

- Capitalize: Nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, and the first and last words of a title.
- Lowercase (Minor Words): Articles (a, an, the), short conjunctions (and, but, or, for, nor), and short prepositions (in, on, at, to, of, by, for, with) unless they begin or end the title.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are words like "the", "and", or "in" kept lowercase in titles?

In standard editorial style guides (AP, Chicago, APA), articles, prepositions, and conjunctions are kept lowercase unless they are the first or last word of the headline.

What is the difference between AP and Chicago title style?

AP style lowercases prepositions of three or fewer letters, whereas Chicago style keeps prepositions lowercase regardless of length unless emphasized or first/last.
