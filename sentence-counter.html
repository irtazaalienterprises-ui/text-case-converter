Why Sentence Counting Matters

Monitoring your sentence count and average sentence length is key to clear communication. Sentences that are too long can confuse readers, while overly short sentences may sound fragmented. Aiming for an average of 15-20 words per sentence ensures optimal readability.

How Sentences Are Identified

Our sentence counter uses smart pattern matching to detect sentence terminators such as periods (.), question marks (?), and exclamation points (!), while properly skipping common abbreviations like Mr., Dr., e.g., and decimal points.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the counter treat abbreviations like "Dr." or "Mr."?

Common abbreviations are recognized and excluded so they do not trigger false sentence counts.

What is an ideal average sentence length?

For general audiences and web content, an average of 14 to 18 words per sentence is recommended for clarity and readability.
