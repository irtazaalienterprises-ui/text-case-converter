What is a Line Break Remover?

A line break remover eliminates unwanted line returns, paragraph breaks, and hard wraps from text copied out of PDF files, emails, or terminal logs, converting fragments back into continuous paragraphs.

Why Use Line Break Remover?

- Fix PDF Copy-Pasting: PDFs often insert forced hard breaks at the end of every column line.
- Custom Delimiters: Convert lists into comma-separated strings (`item1, item2, item3`).
- Preserve Paragraphs: Removes single line breaks while keeping paragraph blocks intact.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I preserve paragraph breaks while fixing line breaks?

Ensure "Preserve Paragraph Breaks" is checked. This keeps double newlines while removing single line wraps within paragraphs.

Can I convert a column list into a comma-separated list?

Yes. Select the "Comma & Space" option, and every line in your list will be joined into a comma-separated sentence.
