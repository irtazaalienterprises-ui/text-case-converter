What is a Text Reverser?

A text reverser flips text, word sequence, or line order backward. It can be used for ciphering messages, reversing list items, creating palindromes, or writing upside-down text for social media.

Available Reversal Modes

- Reverse Characters: Flips every single letter backward (e.g. Hello → olleH).
- Reverse Words: Keeps letters within words intact but reverses word order (e.g. Hello World → World Hello).
- Reverse Lines: Reverses line sequence from bottom to top.
- Flip Upside Down: Converts characters into upside-down Unicode symbols (e.g. uʍop ǝpısd∩).
