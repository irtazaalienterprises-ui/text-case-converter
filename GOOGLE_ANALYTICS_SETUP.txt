===================================================================
Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Setup Guide for theconvertertool.com
===================================================================

STATUS:
No Google Analytics tracking code is currently embedded on this website.
In accordance with production deployment guidelines, no fake IDs or placeholder
scripts have been added.

WHEN YOU ARE READY TO ADD GOOGLE ANALYTICS:

1. Obtain your official GA4 Measurement ID from your Google Analytics account:
   - Format: G-XXXXXXXXXX

2. Where to Insert:
   Add the standard Google Tag script directly inside the <head> section
   (immediately after the opening <head> tag) of all HTML pages (index.html,
   all tool pages, and 404.html):

   <!-- Google tag (gtag.js) -->
   <script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"></script>
   <script>
     window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
     function gtag(){dataLayer.push(arguments);}
     gtag('js', new Date());

     gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX');
   </script>

3. Replace both instances of "G-XXXXXXXXXX" with your actual Measurement ID.

4. Testing & Verification:
   Open Google Analytics > Realtime report and navigate to https://theconvertertool.com/
   to verify real-time user session tracking.
